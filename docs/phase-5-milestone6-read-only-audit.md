# Phase 5 Milestone 6 read-only audit and proposed implementation plan

Updated: 2026-10-23. Source inspected at Phase 5 checkpoint `a950bf2ddea913369e2dc67c756cb4a86f849d24`. This is a read-only audit and plan. No application code, generated pages, screenshot references, or Lighthouse data were changed.

## Scope and evidence

This audit combines the existing Chromium measurements in [the effective scaling audit](phase-5-effective-scaling-audit.md), the French/English pre-Milestone-5 Hero comparison recorded in `PROJECT-STATUS.md`, and a source-diff check from the Milestone 4 ticket checkpoint `284712167edc18af9f96f574b4f7e0dd97f1ae5a` to the current source. The M5 diff changes desktop Hero bottom-group ownership and desktop layout-width calculations; it does not change tablet Hero stage scaling, About stage/card rules, or Footer font writes. The tablet Hero behavior below is therefore a pre-existing issue; the About and Footer scaling also predate M5 and are not new regressions.

The prior browser audit ran the generated French `/` and English `/en/` routes in Chromium at desktop, tablet portrait, tablet landscape, short-landscape, narrow-phone and short-phone viewports. The measured sizes and transforms match across locales; translated copy can wrap differently. The Hero comparison used the same clean-browser profile and viewport for the pre-M5 and M5 code checkpoints and found no newly introduced tablet/mobile Hero issue.

### Structure, localization, and competing owners

- The generated documents declare `lang="fr"` at `/` and `lang="en"` at `/en/`. About has three intentional authored layout shells (`#apropos`, `#apropos-t`, and `#apropos-m`) with five records each. The three compositions and their animation paths differ; M6 should preserve all five localized records in each active shell and avoid merging markup during this migration.
- `fit()` is the single viewport/profile owner. It currently writes stage transforms, dimensions, tablet Hero title variables, and calls `renderAbout()`/`fitFooter()`. `renderAbout()` owns card grid placement and scroll animation; `fitFooter()` owns footer geometry and currently its font writes. `renderFooter()` owns only scroll-based curtain progress and navbar ink. No second About or Footer controller was found in the current source path.
- The delayed calls at 200, 800, and 2000ms in the main controller are startup refits for fonts/runtime initialization; resize and orientation changes also invoke `fit()`. M6 should verify that only real dimension/profile changes alter geometry and should not add another timer, observer, or cross-section dependency.
- The current Hero title's no-wrap spans and scaled position/width explain the known tablet clipping/alignment defect. Forcing the whole tablet title to wrap without correcting its containing width/gutter would leave the underlying mismatch unresolved.
- Footer legal dialogs are outside the footer section and have their own `fitSheets()` behavior. Keep their geometry/typography as an explicitly scoped follow-up for Milestone 9/10 unless focused evidence shows they block Footer acceptance; do not fold an unrelated dialog redesign into M6.

## Proposed bounded implementation sequence

1. **M6.1 — Freeze a measurement contract.** Re-run focused runtime probes in French and English at 834×1112 and 1032×1376 (portrait), 1194×834 and 1024×600 (landscape/short landscape), 560×800 and 659×834 (narrow tablet), plus 1440×900 and 1100×700 (desktop). Record CSS font size, ancestor scale, effective size, title bounds, shared gutters, active profile, clipping/overlap, About card bounds, five-record order, footer text bounds, and section transitions. These are review measurements, not new screenshot baselines.
2. **M6.2 — Tablet Hero.** Keep `tHeroRef` image/slideshow fitting and loop lifecycle. Remove the title's dependency on the stage scale variables, give the title a fluid available width based on the shared tablet gutter, and permit natural bilingual wrapping. Position the portrait and cue against the same visible gutter without scaling the cue text; retain a 12px effective cue. Preserve the existing image crop, slide sequence, scroll cue location, and Hero-to-Projects transition.
3. **M6.3 — Desktop About.** Replace only the text-bearing `aStageRef` layout fit with fluid geometry; preserve the desktop section duration, sticky stage, five card records, colors, positions at the approved 1440 composition, and `renderAbout()` entrance/settled motion. Make card placement responsive without using a scale transform on text.
4. **M6.4 — Tablet About.** Remove the `tAStageRef` scale as the mechanism for fitting content. Adapt statement and card geometry to portrait, landscape, narrow and short screens, while keeping the tablet section/document boundary, five cards, grid ordering, and scroll-driven card animation. Verify all text at intended tablet sizes and prevent clipping/overlap.
5. **M6.5 — Desktop and tablet Footer.** Keep `fitFooter()` as the geometry owner but stop multiplying text font sizes by `s`/`st`. Use independent 56/48px headings, 14px contact text, and 12px legal labels with fluid positioning and wrapping. Preserve the tablet `_fhT` height latch, fixed layout, video loop, legal-link targets, footer entry/curtain progression, and Footer-to-end behavior. Keep the curtain animation-only transform unchanged.
6. **M6.6 — Focused acceptance and visual review.** Run the Hero/About/Footer focused specs in French and English, all listed sizes, direct load and repeated resize/orientation changes. Assert no whole-container text scale, target effective sizes, shared Hero/nav gutters, no clipped title/controls, five About records in canonical order, footer readability, preserved media and links, no section-boundary/curtain regressions. Review expected layout changes separately from defects; record implementation, tests, and the user's visual approval independently in the existing 12-area matrix. Do not run the full eight-profile suite or Lighthouse here.

## Milestone 6 acceptance criteria

- Tablet Hero title, portrait, and cue align to the active navbar gutter across 500–1079px tablet-width layouts; title wraps naturally in French and English with no right clipping. At the practical tablet sizes, heading is effectively 48px and cue 12px; the slideshow, background, media loop and interaction remain intact.
- Desktop About remains faithful to its approved 1440px composition and uses effective 56px statement, 40px project/about titles, and 14px supporting text at narrower desktop widths. Tablet About uses effective 48/32/14px typography at portrait, landscape, narrow and short sizes. No text becomes smaller because an About container is transformed.
- Desktop and tablet Footer use effective 56px and 48px headings respectively, 14px contact copy, and 12px legal labels; no JavaScript font-size write reduces those values. Text wraps without overlap/clipping in both locales.
- All five About records and localized content remain present in each preserved shell. Card animation order, project-to-About handoff, Footer entry, video loop, legal links, and curtain progress remain functional.
- No change to tablet Projects tickets/rail, mobile layouts, approved screenshots, asset files, or Lighthouse data during M6.
- Focused automated checks pass in both languages; visual approval remains separately pending for the Hero tablet, About desktop/tablet, and Footer desktop/tablet matrix areas. Milestone 7 remains untouched.

## Risk and recovery

The highest risk is preserving the sticky-scroll timing while removing a scale that currently defines both a design coordinate system and text size. About stage/card animation and the section boundary must therefore be captured before each change and validated after each area. Footer text currently uses inline styles, so removing just the font multipliers must be verified against its absolute placement and the iPad height latch. Keep one small recovery commit for the tablet Hero, one for desktop About, one for tablet About, and one for Footer; if a stage's scroll geometry cannot be preserved with local fluid layout, stop and report the specific dependency before introducing another controller or measurement workaround.

French and English mobile Lighthouse TBT remain unresolved release blockers. No performance investigation or optimization is included in Milestone 6.
